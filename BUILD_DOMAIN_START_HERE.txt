                        WHAT IS BUILD DOMAIN?
================================================================================

Creator economy platform where:

USERS:
  - 10-10,000 creators build & sell digital products
  - Teams collaborate on projects
  - Marketplace enables discovery & monetization

FEATURES:
  - Projects (collaborative workspaces)
  - Sprints (time-boxed cycles)
  - Tasks (Kanban board)
  - Marketplace (monetized creations)
  - Revenue sharing (80-95% to creator)
  - Downstream income (passive from derivatives)
  - XP/Levels (gamification)

TECH:
  - Frontend: 6 Web Components (no framework)
  - Backend: 17 Netlify Functions
  - Database: 10 tables in Supabase
  - Real-time: Supabase Realtime WebSockets
  - Payment: Stripe Connect
