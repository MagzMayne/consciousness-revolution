Understanding recall
Recall ensures sellers survive hardship, while buyers receive value back once stability is confirmed. You’ll see status updates and receive on-chain memos.
Guided terms & fees
Simple steps
  • List: Seller enters asset details and gets fair retail and discounted listing.
  • Agree: Buyer reviews terms (discount, insurance window), then pays via SPL or PayPal.
  • Log: On-chain memo anchors the deal; metadata stored off-server.
  • Protect: If hardship hits, seller can invoke recall; vault coordinates settlement.
  • Settle: Buyer value is returned after stability confirmation within the insurance window.
Fees and splits
  • SPL split: Example split 95% to seller, 5% to vault reserve (configurable).
  • USD conversion: PayPal USD→crypto fee shown before handoff; buyer can accept or cancel.
  • Storage: Vault intake may include shipping and handling fees (quoted via email).
Eligibility rules
  • Double collateral: Seller must hold ≥ 2× item value in SPL balance to enable transmutation and vault storage.
  • Authenticity: High-value listings require lab certifications to maximize confidence.
  • Insurance window: Recall and buyer value return occur within the agreed window.
