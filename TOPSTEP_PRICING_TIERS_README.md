## Trading Strategies Explained

### 1. Mean Reversion Strategy
- **Win Rate:** 65%
- **Average Trade:** $45
- **Method:** Bollinger Bands with RSI confirmation
- **Best Market:** Ranging/sideways markets
- **Entry:** Price touches outer bands with oversold/overbought RSI
- **Exit:** Price returns to middle band or opposite band

### 2. Momentum Breakout Strategy
- **Win Rate:** 58%
- **Average Trade:** $85
- **Method:** Breakout above/below key levels with volume
- **Best Market:** Trending markets
- **Entry:** Strong breakout with ADX > 25 and volume confirmation
- **Exit:** Trailing stop or momentum exhaustion

### 3. Adaptive Regime Strategy
- **Win Rate:** 68%
- **Average Trade:** $95
- **Method:** Market regime detection + automatic strategy switching
- **Best Market:** All market conditions
- **Entry:** Based on detected market regime (trending/ranging/volatile)
- **Exit:** Dynamic based on current regime

### 4. Grid Trading Strategy (Elite Only)
- **Win Rate:** 72%
- **Average Trade:** $65
- **Method:** Multiple orders at different price levels
- **Best Market:** Ranging markets with volatility
- **Entry:** Grid of buy/sell orders above and below current price
- **Exit:** Take profit on each grid level or full position closure

### 5. Multi-Timeframe Strategy (Elite Only)
- **Win Rate:** 70%
- **Average Trade:** $105
- **Method:** Analyze multiple timeframes for confluence
- **Best Market:** All markets with clear trends
- **Entry:** When multiple timeframes align (5m, 15m, 1h, 4h)
- **Exit:** Based on highest timeframe signal reversal

### 6. Custom Strategy Builder (Elite Only)
- Build and backtest your own strategies
- Combine multiple indicators
- Set custom entry/exit rules
- Real-time performance tracking
