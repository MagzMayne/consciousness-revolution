Safety & respect

No doxxing, no blame, no “hunts.” Focus on care, observation, and accurate reporting.

  • Do not share exact home addresses or private contact details.
  • Do not accuse or name suspected perpetrators.
  • Do not attempt to track or confront anyone yourself.
  • Share only what you know first‑hand or from trusted, public sources.
