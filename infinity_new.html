GEAR UP - Take ARAYA With You

You just met your AI ally. Now make sure you can access her anytime.

Why an extension? ARAYA works best when she is always within reach. The browser extension puts her in a sidepanel that stays with you across every website. Reading an article? Ask ARAYA to summarize. Stuck on a problem? She is right there.

Code capture. The extension can also capture code snippets from any webpage and save them to your personal library. Build your knowledge base as you browse.

Option 1: Bookmark (Quick)

Save ARAYA to your browser bookmarks for instant access.

  1. Press Ctrl+D (Win) or Cmd+D (Mac)
  2. Name it "ARAYA" and save
  3. Access her from your bookmarks bar
