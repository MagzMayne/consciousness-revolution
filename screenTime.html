3. Monthly contribution & reward eligibility

Each month, you opt-in to contribute your locally computed metrics. These drive the reward engine and the public leaderboard.

Contributing each month keeps you eligible for dividend payouts and leaderboard bonuses. Your contribution combines:

  • Screen time hours logged
  • Estimated value created from that time
  • Value of insights extracted from your anonymized metrics
Prototype & ethical guardrails
  • This page is a front-end prototype only. No real payments or payouts are processed here.

How bonuses work

The reward engine allocates a monthly pool proportional to:

  • Hours of screen time tracked
  • Quality and diversity of activity categories
  • Value of insights derived from aggregated metrics

If you reach the top portion of the leaderboard in any month, you receive a lifetime bonus tier. Even if your ranking changes later, that multiplier stays attached to your account.
