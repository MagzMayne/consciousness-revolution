⚙️ Motor Configuration - DIY Kits

Complete DIY kits include 4 total stepper motors with specialized functions:

  • X-axis motor: Moves the stone to and away from the wheel
  • Y-axis motor: Moves the stone up and down
  • Angle control motor: Includes planetary gear box for precise angle adjustments
  • Index motor: Specialized motor with 96 steps per revolution for facet positioning

🏗️ Technical Specifications

Hardware Components

  • 🔩 2020 Aluminum Extrusion Frame
  • ⚙️ 96-Tooth Index Gear for precise positioning
  • 🔄 Ball Screw X & Y Axis (100mm travel each)
  • 🎯 4 Stepper Motors:
    • • X-axis motor (moves stone to/away from wheel)
    • • Y-axis motor (moves stone up/down)
    • • Angle control motor (with planetary gear box)
    • • Specialized index motor (96 steps per revolution)

  • 🤖 Arduino Controller with Motor Shields
