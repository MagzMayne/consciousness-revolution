USPS CERTIFIED MAIL INSTRUCTIONS
What to say and do at the post office

========================================
WHAT YOU NEED TO BRING
========================================
- 13 sealed letters (Notice of Default inside)
- This instruction sheet
- Payment method (~$170 total)
- Phone to photograph receipts

========================================
AT THE COUNTER - SAY THIS
========================================

FOR DOMESTIC (10 letters - USA addresses):
"I need Certified Mail with Return Receipt
Requested for these 10 letters."

Cost: ~$7 each = ~$70 total

FOR INTERNATIONAL (3 letters - Vatican):
"I need Registered Mail International with
Return Receipt for these 3 letters to
Vatican City."

IMPORTANT: Say "Vatican City" NOT "Italy"
The postal code is 00120 Vatican City

Cost: ~$25-35 each = ~$100 total

========================================
WHAT YOU'LL RECEIVE
========================================

1. GREEN CARDS (Return Receipt)
   - One for each letter
   - Gets mailed back to you when delivered
   - THIS IS YOUR PROOF

2. TRACKING NUMBERS
   - One receipt per letter
   - Save ALL of these
   - Use USPS.com to track

3. POSTMARK RECEIPT
   - Shows date sent
   - Keep for records

========================================
AFTER SENDING
========================================

1. PHOTOGRAPH everything
   - All receipts
   - All tracking numbers
   - Green cards when they return

2. TRACK ONLINE
   - Go to USPS.com/track
   - Enter each tracking number
   - Screenshot delivery confirmations

3. REPORT TO DISCORD
   - Post tracking numbers in #jason-project
   - Post when deliveries confirmed
   - Upload receipt photos

========================================
TIMELINE
========================================

Domestic: 3-7 business days
International: 14-21 business days

30-DAY CLOCK starts when DELIVERED
(not when sent)

========================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
========================================

Q: They ask if I want insurance?
A: No, just Certified Mail with Return Receipt

Q: They say Vatican is Italy?
A: Vatican City is a separate country.
   Postal code 00120, NOT an Italian code.

Q: Can I do this online?
A: No. Certified Mail must be done in person.

Q: What if they won't sign?
A: USPS records delivery attempts.
   Multiple attempts = proof of service.

========================================
COSTS SUMMARY
========================================

10 Domestic Letters @ ~$7    = ~$70
3 Vatican Letters @ ~$33     = ~$100
                     TOTAL   = ~$170

(Prices may vary slightly by location)
