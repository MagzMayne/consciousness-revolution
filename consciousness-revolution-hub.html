How It Works

STEP 01
Launch Your Node
Start free on EzAutobots. Set up and run a compute node to join the network. Earn base (1×) compute and task rewards immediately — no subscription required.
STEP 02
Choose Your Access Tier
Scale your impact with a paid tier. Builder (1.5×) → Automator (2×) → Engineer (3×) → Core Dev (5×). Each tier unlocks more powerful dashboards and a higher reward multiplier.
STEP 03
Build & Collaborate
Browse 1450+ projects across both domains. Submit issues, pull requests, or whole new features. Review peers' work and share knowledge — everyone grows faster together.
STEP 04
Earn Escalating Rewards
Your reward multiplier scales with your tier (1×→5×). Compute rewards, task bounties, and platform income are all distributed based on your tier and participation score.
