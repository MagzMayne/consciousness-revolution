📋 What IS This Dashboard?

A personal command center organized into 7 life domains:

  • COMMAND - Your daily priorities and control center
  • CRAFT - What you're building and creating
  • CONNECT - Relationships and communication
  • GUARD - Protection, boundaries, security
  • SCALE - Business growth, money, expansion
  • ACADEMY - Learning, skills, development
  • INFINITY - Big picture vision, consciousness

Each domain has 3 tiers: Personal (private), Team (shared), Public (visible to world).

✅ What WORKS Right Now

These features are tested and functional:

  • View your 7 domains - Click tabs, see each area
  • Track simple metrics - Click "+1" buttons to count things
  • Health scores - See percentage readouts (based on your clicks)
  • Navigation - Press keys 1-7 to switch domains
  • Forge Quick Access - Jump to any of the 7 Forges
  • Team Tasks page - See shared task list
  • Team Comms - Basic team communication
  • Help page - Instructions for what you CAN do
  • Ask ARAYA - Chat with AI for guidance
  • Bug Reporter - Submit issues and requests

❌ What DOESN'T Work Yet

Be honest - these features are NOT ready:

  • Custom widgets - You can't add your own buttons yet
  • Email integration - Can't connect your email
  • Project lists - Can't add your own projects
  • Things for sale - No e-commerce integration
  • Dashboard editing - You're stuck with what we give you
  • Real analytics - The readouts are based on manual clicks, not real data
  • Auto-sync - Changes don't sync across devices automatically
  • ARAYA dashboard editing - AI can't modify your dashboard yet

🔧 How to Request Changes

Since you can't edit your dashboard directly yet, here's how to get changes:

  1. Use Bug Reporter - Go to bugs.html and describe what you want
  2. Ask ARAYA - Describe your request, we'll see it in the logs
  3. Team Comms - Post your request for the team to see
  4. Wait for Commander - Changes go live when Commander reviews commits

⚠️ This is manual and slow. We know. Working on automation.
