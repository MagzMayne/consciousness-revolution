Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about drywall, plaster, and ceiling repair.

How long does a typical drywall repair take? >
Most repairs take 1–3 hours depending on size, location, and drying time. Larger or multi‑step repairs may require a return visit for sanding and painting.
Can you match my existing texture? >
Yes — texture matching is one of our specialties. We carefully blend new work into your existing walls or ceilings so the repair disappears.
Do you repair water‑damaged ceilings? >
Absolutely. We remove damaged material, replace it with new drywall or plaster, and match the finish and paint so it looks like nothing ever happened.
Do you work on older homes with plaster and lath? >
Yes — we specialize in early‑century plaster restoration and lath repair. We understand how to stabilize and restore older materials safely and effectively.
Do you offer free quotes? >
Yes. Call or text anytime for a fast, free estimate. You can also send photos through our online booking form for a quick quote range.
Do you work evenings or weekends? >
We offer flexible scheduling and 24/7 availability for quotes. Let us know your timing needs and we’ll do our best to accommodate.
Do you handle small jobs? >
Yes — no job is too small. From nail pops and small holes to full room repairs, we’re happy to help.
