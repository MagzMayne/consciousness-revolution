    Root of the Idea
    PeaceAI was born from a simple truth: one person cannot be everywhere at once, but care and stewardship still demand constant attention. Churches, especially, carry the weight of community, safety, and sacred responsibility. Ryan saw his father carrying that weight daily — checking doors, watching cameras, worrying about lights, walking the grounds long after everyone else had gone home.

    Instead of replacing that vigilance, PeaceAI was created to honor it. A tool built to be simple, quiet, and faithful — a companion that watches the building so the steward can watch over the people. No complexity. No clutter. Just a clear, dependable layer of intelligence that turns every camera into a guardian, every alert into clarity, and every night into peace of mind.

    Simplicity of the Tool
    PeaceAI is intentionally minimal:
    • It discovers the cameras automatically.
    • It analyzes everything on‑site through a Python vision engine.
    • It alerts only the right people, only when it matters.
    • It locks doors, turns off lights, and summarizes the day without being asked.
    • It requires no technical skill — only trust.
