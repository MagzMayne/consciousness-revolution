What is the Healthcare Domain?

This domain helps you navigate the complex healthcare system with awareness and confidence. Healthcare decisions can be overwhelming, expensive, and sometimes manipulated by financial incentives. These tools help you evaluate medical advice objectively, question authority when appropriate, and make informed decisions about your health.

Use this domain to:

  • Evaluate Medical Advice: Question recommendations and verify claims independently
  • Detect Financial Conflicts: Identify when profit motives may influence treatment recommendations
  • Make Informed Decisions: Weigh options systematically without emotional manipulation
  • Advocate for Yourself: Communicate effectively with healthcare providers about your needs

Why it matters: Your health is too important to blindly trust authority figures who may have conflicting incentives. These tools help you be an informed participant in your healthcare, asking the right questions and making decisions aligned with your actual needs—not someone else's agenda.
