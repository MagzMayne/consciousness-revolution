# Rdata: The Freedom Quest - World Peace Game Guide

## Overview

Rdata has been transformed into a **never-ending game** where every person on Earth can become a peace agent working toward the ultimate goal: **World Peace**. Complete missions, track global progress, and help unite humanity in a shared quest for peace.

## 🎮 Game Concept

### The Ultimate Goal
**100% World Peace** - achieved when every person on Earth (8.1 billion) becomes an active player.

### Never-Ending Gameplay
- Missions generate infinitely
- No game over state
- Continuous progress tracking
- Perpetual engagement

### Real-World Integration
- Virtual missions using NASA data
- Real-world scavenger hunts
- Community humanitarian actions
- Location-based challenges

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Open the Game
Navigate to `rdata.html` in your browser or visit the live site.

### Step 2: Join the Quest
1. Look for the **Player Profile** panel (top left)
2. Click the **"Join the Quest"** button
3. You'll receive:
   - Unique Agent ID (e.g., PA-LQ8N9O-X7K2)
   - Random agent role
   - Your first mission automatically

### Step 3: Accept Missions
- Your first mission appears in the **Mission Control** panel (bottom left)
- Click **"Get New Mission"** to receive more (max 5 active)
- Read the objective and point value

### Step 4: Complete Missions
- Follow the mission objective
- Click **"Complete Mission"** when done
- Earn peace points
- Watch global progress increase

## 📈 Progression System

### Peace Points
- Earned by completing missions
- Point values:
  - Global Awareness: 10-30 points
  - Cooperation: 20-40 points
  - Scavenger Hunt: 30-50 points
  - Humanitarian: 40-60 points
  - Peace Initiative: 50-70 points

### Mission Completion
- Each mission increases your completion count
- Contributes to global peace progress
- No limit on total missions

### Global Progress
The World Peace Progress percentage increases based on:
- Total number of active players
- Participation rate (players / world population)
- Collective mission completions

Formula: `(Players / 8.1B × 100) + mission impact`

## ❓ FAQ

### Do I need an account?
No! Everything is saved in your browser. No registration beyond clicking "Join the Quest."

### Is my data shared?
No. All data stays in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to servers.

### Can I play offline?
Yes! The game works offline. Only NASA API features require internet.

### How do I reset my progress?
Clear your browser's localStorage or use incognito mode for a fresh start.

### Can I have multiple profiles?
Use different browsers or clear localStorage to start fresh.

### Do missions expire?
No. Active missions stay until completed or you clear them.

### What if I complete all 40+ missions?
Missions randomize, so you can complete them multiple times. Each completion counts.

### How do real-world missions work?
Currently on honor system. Click "Complete" when done. Photo verification coming soon.

### Why isn't the player count increasing faster?
It's a simulation showing gradual growth. Real growth depends on actual players joining.

### Will there be prizes?
Currently, the reward is contributing to world peace and personal satisfaction. Future features may include digital rewards.
