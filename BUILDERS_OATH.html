The 7 Hermetic Principles

Ancient wisdom that predates every religion. The operating system of reality itself.

I

The Principle of Mentalism

"The All is Mind; the Universe is Mental."

Your thoughts shape your reality. Change your mind, change your world.
II

The Principle of Correspondence

"As above, so below; as below, so above."

Patterns repeat at every scale. Master one domain, understand all domains.
III

The Principle of Vibration

"Nothing rests; everything moves; everything vibrates."

Your energy is contagious. Raise your frequency, raise everyone around you.
IV

The Principle of Polarity

"Everything is dual; opposites are identical in nature, but different in degree."

Hot and cold are the same thing. Love and hate are the same thing. Change the degree.
V

The Principle of Rhythm

"Everything flows, out and in; everything has its tides."

Good times follow bad. Bad times follow good. Ride the waves instead of fighting them.
VI

The Principle of Cause and Effect

"Every cause has its effect; every effect has its cause."

Nothing happens by chance. Trace back far enough and you find the cause. Become the cause.
VII

The Principle of Gender

"Gender is in everything; everything has its masculine and feminine principles."

Creation requires both giving and receiving. Building requires both action and nurturing.
