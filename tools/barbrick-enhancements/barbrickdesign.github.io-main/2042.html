CCART — 3D Holographic Rotating Card
Works on mobile and desktop. Create offline, sign later with Phantom. Touch/mouse/tilt controls.
Upload an image, render an animated holographic card, archive locally. Phantom optional.
Tip: Tap the card to enable device-tilt. Use two fingers to pinch/zoom on mobile. Page scrolling is preserved when not interacting.
