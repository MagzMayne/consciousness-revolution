### Fill Out:

- ✅ Brief bio about your skills

## FAQ

**Q: How long until I earn money?**  
A: Grant applications take 3-6 months to process. Once funded, you receive monthly distributions.

**Q: Can I upgrade my tier?**  
A: Yes! Email BarbrickDesign@gmail.com to pay the difference and upgrade.

**Q: What if I'm not a coder?**  
A: Many roles available: testing, documentation, design, project management, research.

**Q: How many projects should I join?**  
A: Start with 1-2, add more as you get comfortable. Platinum users often join 5-10.

**Q: When do I get paid?**  
A: Monthly distributions after grant funding is received and processed.

**Q: Can I lose money?**  
A: No! Your initial donation gives you access. You can only earn more, never lose.

**Q: What if a grant isn't funded?**  
A: Apply to multiple grants. System makes it easy. Only pay once for access to all opportunities.

**Q: How do I verify I'm a student?**  
A: Use .edu email OR send student ID to BarbrickDesign@gmail.com with your registration.
