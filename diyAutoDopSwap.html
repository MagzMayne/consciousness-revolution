How the Dop‑Swap System Works

  1. Mount: You mount a NEMA‑17 stepper near your index head and attach the electromagnet and printed faceplate to its shaft.
  2. Rack: You mount the printed dop rack (ring or line) so each dop slot aligns with a specific motor angle.
  3. Wire: You connect Arduino, DRV8825, electromagnet, and (optionally) sensors following the wiring diagrams.
  4. Upload: You open the provided Arduino sketch, set your dop positions, and upload via USB.
  5. Activate: You enter your activation key; the firmware unlocks dop‑swap features for your kit.
  6. Run: Your Gem Bot rotates to a dop slot, energizes the magnet to pick up or drop a dop, and continues faceting.

All position logic is handled in code; you only define how many dop slots you have and which angles/steps they map to.
