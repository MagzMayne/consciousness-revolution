📺 TV/Movie Audio Setup (8+ feet distance)
1. Set Microphone Gain to 170-200% for distant audio
2. Enable Auto Gain Control (recommended for TV)
3. Disable Noise Suppression & Echo Cancellation for clearer TV audio
4. Position device 6-10 feet from TV speakers, pointing at TV
5. Watch Audio Level meter - should show green bars when TV is playing
📱 Phone Speaker Audio Setup (3-6 feet distance)
1. Set Microphone Gain to 140-170% for phone speakers
2. Enable Auto Gain Control for better phone audio
3. Position device 3-6 feet from phone speaker
4. Increase phone volume to 70-80% for clear audio
5. System will auto-detect phone audio and optimize settings
