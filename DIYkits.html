What happens after you subscribe?
How the mesh works behind the scenes
  1. Your subscription is registered and routed to our core distribution dashboard.
  2. The system calculates how many kits and which parts are needed this month.
  3. Trusted builders claim assembly jobs, build your kit, and mark it ready to ship.
  4. You receive a tracked package with parts, instructions, and a new set of projects.
All payments are processed via PayPal to barbrickdesign@gmail.com. You’ll receive a confirmation email from PayPal and from us.
