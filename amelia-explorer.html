Frequently Asked Questions

What age is this for?

Amelia's Explorer Kit is designed for kids ages 8-14. Younger kids can participate with parent help, and older teens often enjoy the projects too!

Do I need any experience?

No experience needed! Issue 1 starts from absolute zero. Each issue builds progressively on previous skills.

Is this safe for kids?

Yes! All components operate at low voltage (3.3V-5V). No soldering required. We recommend adult supervision for kids under 10.

What's the AI companion?

Think Tamagotchi meets tutor! Our ARAYA-powered AI friend remembers your child's builds, celebrates victories, provides hints, and evolves based on their interests.

Why two magazines?

Learning is better with friends! One for your child, one to share. Build together, compare projects, trade collector cards.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes! No contracts, no hassle. Cancel anytime from your account dashboard. Keep all hardware you've received.

When does it ship?

Pre-orders ship when we reach 100 subscribers. Expected launch: Q2 2026. You won't be charged until we ship.

International shipping?

US only at launch. Canada coming Q2, UK/EU/Australia planned for Year 2.
