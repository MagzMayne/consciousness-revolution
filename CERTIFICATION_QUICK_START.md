### Step 4: Take the Assessment

When you've met the requirements:
1. You'll be notified you're ready
2. Schedule your certification assessment
3. Choose assessment format (multiple choice, project, mixed)
4. Complete within time limit
5. Receive immediate feedback

**Assessment Details:**

- Passing score: 80-90% depending on certification

- Proctoring: Optional for high-value certifications

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Are these certifications accredited?**  
A: Currently self-issued, but aligned with industry standards. Seeking formal accreditation partnerships.

**Q: Do employers recognize these certifications?**  
A: Growing employer partner network. Each certification maps to real job requirements and salary ranges.

**Q: How much do certifications cost?**  
A: First certification often free, then $47-97 per assessment attempt.

**Q: How long are certifications valid?**  
A: Typically 3 years, then renewable through continuing education.

**Q: Can I take assessments multiple times?**  
A: Yes, typically 3 attempts per certification with 24-hour cooldown between attempts.

**Q: Are certifications refundable?**  
A: Assessment fees are non-refundable, but you can retake if you don't pass.

**Q: Can I verify certifications for free?**  
A: Yes! Public verification is completely free at `/certifications-verify.html`

**Q: How do I add certifications to LinkedIn?**  
A: Add under "Licenses & Certifications" with verification URL.

**Q: What if my certification expires?**  
A: Take renewal assessment (usually shorter/cheaper) to extend validity.

**Q: Can companies create custom certifications?**  
A: Yes! Enterprise tier allows custom certification development.
