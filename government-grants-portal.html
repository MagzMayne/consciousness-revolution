🎯 The Shotgun Effect Explained

When you donate and become a contributor, your name is added to our pool of credible supporters. Each grant application we submit can include multiple donor/contributor names, which:

  • Increases Credibility: Multiple supporters demonstrate widespread interest and viability
  • Shows Community Impact: Reviewers see diverse stakeholder involvement
  • Demonstrates Sustainability: Multiple funding sources indicate long-term project health
  • Multiplies Success Rate: Applications with 10+ supporters have 3-5x higher approval rates

Your donation doesn't just give you access - it makes ALL applications more successful, creating a rising tide that lifts all boats! 🚀
