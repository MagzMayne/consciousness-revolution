## 🎓 The Learning-to-Earning Path

### Step 1: Learn in the Academy

Start your journey by completing courses in the GemBot Academy:

1. **Basics Course** (📚 GemBot Basics)
   - Introduction to Gemstones
   - Understanding Your GemBot
   - Safety Guidelines
   
2. **Cutting Fundamentals** (✂️ Cutting Fundamentals)
   - Facet Angles
   - Cutting Sequence
   - Table Size & Proportion
   - Girdle Formation

3. **Polishing Mastery** (✨ Polishing Mastery)
   - Polish Compounds
   - Lap Speed Control
   - Scratch Removal
   - Mirror Polish Achievement

4. **Advanced Designs** (🎨 Advanced Designs)
   - Fancy Shapes
   - Custom Faceting
   - Fantasy Cuts
   - Concave Cutting

### Step 2: Earn Certifications

As you complete courses, you'll automatically earn certifications:

- 🎓 **Basic Lapidary** (After Basics)
  - Unlocks: Cabochon, Basic Polish, Simple Grinding
  - Opens: Beginner-level jobs ($25-50 each)

- 💎 **Faceting Fundamentals** (After Cutting Fundamentals)
  - Unlocks: Faceted Round, Oval Cut, Step Cut
  - Opens: Intermediate jobs ($100-200 each)

- ✨ **Polish Master** (After Polishing Mastery)
  - Unlocks: High Polish, Mirror Finish, Premium Finishing
  - Opens: Advanced jobs ($300-600 each)

- 🎨 **Advanced Designer** (After Advanced Designs)
  - Unlocks: Custom Facets, Fantasy Cuts, Precision Work
  - Opens: Professional jobs ($500-2000 each)

### Step 3: Practice in the Farm Game

Use the GemBot Farm Game to:
- Practice cutting without real-world risk
- Build muscle memory for techniques
- Test different gem types and designs
- Track your speed and quality improvements

Your farm game achievements count toward your professional profile!

## 💰 Earning Real Income

### Finding Jobs

1. **Open the GemLords Dashboard**
   - Look for the "💎 Real Job Marketplace" panel
   - Located in the sidebar or bottom-right of interface
   - Click to expand and view available jobs

2. **Browse Qualified Jobs**
   - Jobs are filtered based on your certifications
   - Green ✅ badge = you're qualified
   - Gray 🔒 badge = complete more academy lessons to unlock

3. **View Job Details**
   - Click "📋 View Details" for full information
   - Review pay, timeline, and requirements
   - Check the source listing on eBay

### Applying for Jobs

1. Click "✅ Apply Now" on any qualified job
2. Your application is submitted to the job provider
3. You'll be notified when accepted (usually within 24-48 hours)
4. Once accepted, rough stone ships to you (insured)

### Completing Jobs

1. **Receive Materials**
   - Insured shipping of rough gemstone
   - Job specifications and requirements
   - Contact info for questions

2. **Cut the Stone**
   - Follow specifications precisely
   - Use techniques learned in academy
   - Document your work with photos
   - Quality matters for your rating!

3. **Ship Back**
   - Insured return shipping provided
   - Include your invoice/documentation
   - Photos of completed work

4. **Get Paid**
   - Quality review by job provider
   - Payment processed within 7 days
   - Money appears in your earnings balance

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How much can I realistically earn?**
A: Beginners: $100-300/month part-time. Intermediate: $500-1000/month. Advanced: $1500-3000/month. Professional: $3000-10000/month.

**Q: Do I need my own GemBot machine?**
A: No! Jobs require gemstone cutting skills, but you can use any equipment. Having a GemBot gives bonuses though.

**Q: What if I make a mistake and damage the stone?**
A: Beginner jobs have insurance. You're only liable for intentional damage or negligence. Accidents happen - that's why we practice first!

**Q: How do taxes work?**
A: You're an independent contractor. Download your annual earnings report for tax filing. Consult a tax professional in your area.

**Q: Can I work on multiple jobs at once?**
A: Yes! Take as many as you can handle. Just meet all deadlines and maintain quality.

**Q: What if a job provider isn't satisfied?**
A: Quality disputes are mediated fairly. Both sides provide evidence. Your reputation is protected against unfair claims.

**Q: Are the eBay listings real?**
A: Yes! GemLords connects to real gemstone rough listings. Job providers purchase rough and hire cutters to add value.

**Q: How do I get my first job?**
A: Complete "Basic Lapidary" certification (3-5 hours of learning). Apply for beginner cabochon jobs. Build from there!
