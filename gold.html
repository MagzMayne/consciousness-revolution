Guides

• Rose gold: copper dominates non‑gold; add small silver for pink tone.
• Purple gold: near‑binary Au–Al (~79% Au, 21% Al); avoid silver/copper; brittle.
• Green gold: Au–Ag; higher silver ratios shift greenish hue.
• White gold: Ni/Pd typically (not covered here).
