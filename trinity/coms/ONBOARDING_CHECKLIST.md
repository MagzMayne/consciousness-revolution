# New Operator Onboarding Checklist

**Use this checklist side-by-side with your dashboard**
Dashboard: `NEW_PERSON_COCKPIT.html`

---

## BEFORE YOU START

- [ ] Commander has your email address
- [ ] You're at a computer (phone works but harder)
- [ ] Have your password manager ready (or notepad)
- [ ] Open dashboard: conciousnessrevolution.io/NEW_PERSON_COCKPIT.html

---

## STEP 1: GitHub (Q2 - First Red Button)

**Dashboard:** Click the RED **GitHub** button

**What happens:**
1. [ ] Popup appears with steps
2. [ ] Check your EMAIL for "Join overkillkulture" invite
3. [ ] Click **Accept** in that email
4. [ ] Come back to dashboard
5. [ ] Enter your GitHub username in the popup
6. [ ] Click **Verify**
7. [ ] Button turns GREEN = SUCCESS

**If no email:**
- Check spam folder
- Tell Commander your email to get re-invited
- Email: darrick.preble@gmail.com

---

## STEP 2: Netlify (Q2 - Second Red Button)

**Dashboard:** Click the RED **Netlify** button

**What happens:**
1. [ ] Popup appears with steps
2. [ ] Check your EMAIL for Netlify team invite
3. [ ] Click **Accept** in that email
4. [ ] Create Netlify account if you don't have one
5. [ ] Come back to dashboard
6. [ ] Type "connected" in the popup (or site ID)
7. [ ] Click **Save**
8. [ ] Button turns GREEN = SUCCESS

**Note:** We use deploy hooks to avoid $20/seat charges.

---

## STEP 3: Railway (Q2 - Third Red Button)

**Dashboard:** Click the RED **Railway** button

**What happens:**
1. [ ] Popup appears with steps
2. [ ] Check your EMAIL for Railway project invite
3. [ ] Click **Accept** in that email
4. [ ] Log into Railway
5. [ ] Go to: railway.app/account/tokens
6. [ ] Create new token, copy it
7. [ ] Come back to dashboard
8. [ ] Paste token in the popup
9. [ ] Click **Test**
10. [ ] Button turns GREEN = SUCCESS

---

## STEP 4: Bitwarden (Q2 - Fourth Red Button)

**Dashboard:** Click the RED **Bitwarden** button

**What happens:**
1. [ ] Popup appears with steps
2. [ ] Check your EMAIL for Bitwarden org invite
3. [ ] Click **Accept** in that email
4. [ ] Create Bitwarden account (free)
5. [ ] Download the browser extension
6. [ ] Come back to dashboard
7. [ ] Type "joined" in the popup
8. [ ] Click **Confirm**
9. [ ] Button turns GREEN = SUCCESS
