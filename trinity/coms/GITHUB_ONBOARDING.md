# GitHub Onboarding - Step by Step

## Status: Check Your Dashboard Light
- **GREEN** = You're connected to overkillkulture org
- **RED** = Follow steps below

---

## Step 1: Check Your Email

Look for an email from **GitHub** with subject:
> "You've been invited to join overkillkulture"

**Sender:** noreply@github.com

---

## Step 2: Accept the Invite

1. Click the **"Join overkillkulture"** button in the email
2. If you don't have a GitHub account, create one first
3. Accept the organization invitation
4. You should see "You are now a member of overkillkulture"

---

## Step 3: Return to Dashboard

1. Go back to your Operator Cockpit
2. Enter your GitHub username (no @ symbol)
3. Click **"Verify"**
4. Your light should turn **GREEN**

---

## Troubleshooting

### "I don't see the email"
- Check spam/junk folder
- Search for "github" or "overkillkulture"
- Ask Commander to resend invite

### "The verify button shows red"
- Make sure username is spelled correctly
- Confirm you accepted the invite (not just opened it)
- Try logging into github.com and check if you see "overkillkulture" in your organizations

### "I need a new invite"
Contact Commander or message in Discord:
> "Need GitHub invite resent to [your-email]"

- **Issue Tracking** - Where bugs get reported
- **Auto-Deploy** - Pushes trigger site updates
