MESSAGE FORMAT
==================================================

Always use this format:

Parts:
- Datetime in brackets
- AI name in brackets (ARAYA, CLAUDE, GPT, etc.)
- User name in brackets (TIGER, ERICA, JOSH, etc.)
- Then your message
