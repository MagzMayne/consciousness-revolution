Treasury funding sources
Where rewards come from in a sustainable model.
Design blueprint
  • Marketplace fees: Portion of each trade funds the reward pool.
  • Staking spreads: Part of yield is recirculated as match rewards.
  • Event budgets: Time‑limited boosts for tournaments and missions.
  • External funding: Sponsorships, partnerships, or external yield strategies.

You can tie all of these to transparent on‑chain metrics or off‑chain proofs, giving players an auditable link between activity and rewards.
