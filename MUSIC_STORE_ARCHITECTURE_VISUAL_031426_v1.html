🏗️ Technical Architecture

Storage Layer (3-Tier)

Netlify Free Tier (Preview Tracks)

Purpose: 10 best tracks in MP3 320kbps (~200MB total)

Use Case: Web player streaming preview

✅ 100GB/mo bandwidth
✅ Already deployed
✅ Global CDN included
⚠️ 500 plays/day limit
⚠️ No analytics included

Cloudflare R2 (Premium Downloads)

Purpose: All 91 tracks in lossless WAV (~2GB total)

Use Case: Paid customer downloads only

✅ $0 egress fees (vs S3)
✅ 10GB free storage
✅ S3-compatible API
⚠️ No streaming (download only)
⚠️ Signed URLs required

Spotify/Apple Music (Free Streaming)

Purpose: All tracks via DistroKid distribution

Use Case: Embedded iframes on DWREK site

✅ Infinite free streaming
✅ Platform credibility
✅ Analytics included
✅ Playlist discovery
⚠️ Low payouts ($0.003/stream)
⚠️ Platform owns audience

Gumroad (Payment & Delivery)

Purpose: Premium track/album sales

Use Case: Direct sales + download delivery

✅ 10% fee (no monthly cost)
✅ Auto download delivery
✅ Email receipts
✅ Sales analytics
⚠️ Higher fee than Stripe
⚠️ Gumroad branding
