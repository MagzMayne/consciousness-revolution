III. Key Legal Issues

Issue 1: Breach by Petitioner - Quit Claim Deed

Per the October 2022 Divorce Decree, Petitioner was required to execute a Quit Claim Deed transferring her interest in the marital residence to Respondent. As of this date, the deed has never been executed despite 2.5 years of Respondent making payments.

Evidence: May 30, 2025 email from Respondent stating "I've been waiting for the quit claim deed for almost two years" and "I've held my end of the bargain."

Issue 2: Perjury Contradiction

A fatal contradiction exists between two documents signed by Petitioner under penalty of perjury:

Document Date Statement Under Oath
Parenting Plan (Page 2, Section 3) October 13, 2022 "Neither parent has any of these problems" - Domestic Violence: NEITHER, Assault: NEITHER, Abuse: NEITHER
Protection Order Petition May 29, 2025 Claims she needs protection from Respondent due to safety concerns

LEGAL CONSEQUENCE

These statements are mutually exclusive. If the 2022 statement was true, the 2025 petition is perjury. If the 2025 petition is true, the 2022 statement was perjury.

RCW 9A.72.020: Perjury in the first degree is a Class B Felony punishable by up to 10 years imprisonment and $20,000 fine.

Issue 3: Retaliation Timeline

The protection order filing timeline suggests retaliation rather than legitimate safety concern:

  • May 29, 2025: Protection Order filed
  • May 30, 2025: Respondent sends email demanding Quit Claim Deed
Pattern: Protection order filed ONE DAY BEFORE Respondent's demand for the legally-owed deed. No safety incident occurred between 2022 and 2025.

Issue 4: Procedural - RCW 4.28.180

Respondent resides in Montana. Under RCW 4.28.180, out-of-state defendants are entitled to 60 days to respond to service. Petitioner's Motion to Enforce allowed only 21 days.

VI. Available Legal Arguments

Defense 1: First Material Breach (Quit Claim Deed)

Under Washington contract law, a party who first materially breaches a contract cannot enforce the other party's obligations. Petitioner's failure to execute the Quit Claim Deed for 2.5 years constitutes first material breach.

Defense 2: Fraud Upon the Court

The perjury contradiction between 2022 and 2025 documents constitutes fraud upon the court. The protection order was obtained through false statements, and any enforcement action relying on that order should be void.

Defense 3: Procedural Violation

RCW 4.28.180 requires 60-day response time for out-of-state defendants. Failure to comply renders the Motion to Enforce procedurally deficient.

Defense 4: Offset/Overpayment

Even if any amount were legitimately owed, Respondent's $8,513+ overpayment serves as complete offset.

Potential Counterclaims

  • Specific performance: Compel execution of Quit Claim Deed
  • Damages for breach of divorce decree
  • Attorney fees under contract provisions
  • Criminal referral for perjury (RCW 9A.72.020)
