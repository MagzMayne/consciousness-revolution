The Never-Ending Quest

A global game where every person on Earth becomes a peace agent. Complete missions, help others, and watch as humanity unites for world peace.

How It Works

• Join as a peace agent and get your unique ID
• Accept missions: virtual, real-world, or scavenger hunts
• Complete missions to earn peace points
• Watch global peace progress grow with each contribution
• Goal: Every person playing = 100% world peace

Mission Types

🌍 Global Awareness: Learn about world events
🤝 Cooperation: Connect with other agents
📍 Scavenger Hunts: Real-world location challenges
❤️ Humanitarian: Help your community
🕊️ Peace Initiatives: Spread kindness & understanding
