Core Manipulation Patterns

DARVO

Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim and Offender. The abuser denies the behavior, attacks the person confronting them, then claims THEY are the victim.

"I never said that. Why are you always attacking me? You're the one who's abusive!"

Gaslighting

Making you question your own reality. They deny things happened, rewrite history, or tell you you're "too sensitive" or "crazy."

"That never happened. You're imagining things. Everyone thinks you're unstable."

Love Bombing

Overwhelming affection early on. Excessive compliments, gifts, attention - designed to create emotional dependency before the mask drops.

"You're my soulmate. I've never felt this way. Let's move in together after 2 weeks."

Word Salad

Circular, confusing arguments. They talk in circles, change topics, contradict themselves - anything to exhaust you and avoid accountability.

"Well if you hadn't... but that's not even... you always... anyway the point is..."
