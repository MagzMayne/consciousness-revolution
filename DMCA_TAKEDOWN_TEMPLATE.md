## Instructions for Use

### When to Use This Template:

1. When you find unauthorized copies of your code on other websites
2. When someone has cloned your repository and republished without permission
3. When your algorithms or implementations appear in commercial products
4. When your documentation or content is copied without attribution

### Before Filing:

1. ✅ Verify you actually own the copyright
2. ✅ Confirm the use is not authorized
3. ✅ Check if fair use might apply (unlikely for substantial copying)
4. ✅ Gather evidence of infringement (screenshots, URLs, comparisons)
5. ✅ Attempt direct contact with infringer first (optional but recommended)

### How to File:

1. Fill in all bracketed sections with specific information
2. Include as much evidence as possible
3. Sign and date the notice
4. Send to the platform's designated DMCA agent
5. Keep copies of all correspondence

### Where to File:

- **GitHub:** https://docs.github.com/en/github/site-policy/dmca-takedown-policy
- **Web Hosts:** Check hosting provider's DMCA policy page
- **Social Media:** Use platform's copyright infringement reporting tools
- **Google:** https://support.google.com/legal/answer/3110420

### After Filing:

1. Monitor for removal (typically 24-48 hours)
2. Be prepared for possible counter-notification
3. Document all responses and actions taken
4. Consider legal action if infringement continues
5. Report to authorities if infringement is willful and for commercial advantage

## Legal Considerations

### Consult an Attorney If:

- The infringement is widespread or systematic
- Significant commercial damage has occurred
- The infringer refuses to comply with takedown
- Counter-notifications are filed
- You need to pursue monetary damages

### Additional Resources:

- U.S. Copyright Office: https://www.copyright.gov
- DMCA Safe Harbor: 17 U.S.C. § 512
- Copyright Infringement: 17 U.S.C. § 501-513
- Electronic Frontier Foundation DMCA Guide: https://www.eff.org/issues/dmca

5. Removal confirmations
6. Counter-notifications (if any)
