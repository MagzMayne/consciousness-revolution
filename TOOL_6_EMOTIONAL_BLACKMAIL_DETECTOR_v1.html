The FOG of Emotional Blackmail

F
Fear - "Something bad will happen if you don't comply"
O

G
Guilt - "You're a bad person if you don't do what I want"

What is Emotional Blackmail?

Emotional blackmail is when someone uses your feelings against you to get what they want. They may threaten, guilt-trip, or create fear to control your behavior. Healthy relationships involve direct requests, not manipulation through FOG.
