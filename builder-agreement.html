Builder Agreement

Before you build with us, understand what we expect from each other. This is a commitment, not a signup form.

What You're Agreeing To

1

Be a Builder, Not a Destroyer

Create value, don't tear others down. Solve problems, don't create them. If you're here to criticize without contributing, this isn't for you.

2

Be Honest

Even when it's uncomfortable. Especially when it's uncomfortable. We can't fix what we can't see.

3

Communicate

If you're stuck, say so. If you're out, say so. Silence is worse than bad news. We'd rather know you can't do something than wonder where you went.

4

Own Your Work

Take credit AND responsibility. When something goes well, celebrate it. When something breaks, own it and fix it.

5

Help Others

The goal is collective success. When someone new shows up, help them. When someone's struggling, support them. We rise together.

6

Protect the Mission

Don't leak, don't sabotage, don't manipulate. If you have concerns, bring them up directly. We handle problems internally, not publicly.

Sign the Agreement

By filling this out, you're saying "I understand and I'm in."
