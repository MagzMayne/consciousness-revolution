You step into Esrever after dinner has “already happened.” The meal is perfect and untouched. Your job isn’t to eat it – it’s to safely un‑serve the dish back into ingredients that the alien chef can reuse.

Esrever economy blueprint
The Esrever economy is reward‑first and undo‑driven. Nothing destructive, nothing harmful – just satisfying reverse actions that clean up, un‑tangle, and rewind the world in your favor.
  • Reverse restaurants: Coordinate un‑meals. Finished dishes revert to ingredients; chefs pay you for each clean reversal.
  • Reverse construction: Un‑build prefab towers into modular components that power upgrades to tools, ships, and social spaces.
  • Reverse combat: Un‑damage enemies and restore peace; former foes unlock shortcuts, lore, and cooperation bonuses.
  • Reverse travel: You always arrive first. Walking “backward” reconstructs the path and grants navigation rewards.

Reversefolk behavioral sampler
Esrever’s citizens are cosmic pranksters with strict internal logic. They appear absurd, but their behavior forms a stable foundation for quests, systems, and punchlines that unfold in reverse.
  • Temporal comedians: They giggle at the punchline several minutes before you say it, then wait politely for you to catch up.
  • Gravity skeptics: Walking upside down is just “taking the scenic route.” Falling upward is considered a stylish entrance.
  • Conversational rewind: NPCs greet you with the goodbye they will say at the end of the loop, hinting at outcomes you haven’t reached.
  • Age inversion: Tiny “newborns” deliver tutorials in formal tones; ancient “elders” race around starting side quests, then forget they did.

Esrever loop architecture
Esrever runs on a loop‑based timeline. Each loop is a pass over the same day, but the causality is reversed: rewards and outcomes appear first, and your job is to reverse‑engineer how the world must have gotten there.
  • Loop 000: You arrive after you leave. The world greets you like a familiar stranger.
  • Loop 001: First un‑meal. You learn how to safely reverse‑serve dishes in Esrever Diner.
  • Loop 002: Un‑built plaza. Towers politely collapse into neat piles of parts for you to reassign.
  • Loop 003: Peace after the fight. Enemies you “will heal later” already act like old friends.
