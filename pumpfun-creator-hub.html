📖 How Creator Rewards Work
1. Build — Complete a project and stamp it with a RootIB tag.
2. Launch — Go to pump.fun/create, name your coin after your idea, paste your project URL in the description.
3. Announce — Use the form on this page to auto-post to Discord. The 188-member community sees it in #crypto-launch.
4. Earn — As the community trades your coin you earn Pump.Fun creator fees. CORE holders also earn from overall platform volume.
5. Scale — More coins → more volume → more CORE value → everyone wins.
