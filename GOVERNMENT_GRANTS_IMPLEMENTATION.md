## 🤖 AI System Features

### Grant Matching Algorithm

**Input Profile**:
- Organization type (startup, university, nonprofit, etc.)
- Focus areas (technology, health, energy, etc.)
- Funding needed ($10K to $100M+)
- Eligibility requirements
- Team qualifications

**AI Processing**:
1. Analyzes profile against 500+ grants
2. Scores each grant 0-100 based on:
   - Category match (30 points)
   - Eligibility match (25 points)
   - Amount match (20 points)
   - Focus area alignment (15 points)
   - Success rate (10 points)
3. Ranks grants by score
4. Estimates effort required (hours)
5. Calculates success probability

**Output**:
- Ranked list of matching grants
- Match scores and reasons
- Estimated success rates
- Required effort per grant
- Application deadlines

### Proposal Generation

**6 Auto-Generated Sections**:

1. **Executive Summary** (150 words)
   - Project overview
   - Funding request
   - Expected impact

2. **Technical Approach** (10 pages)
   - Background & significance
   - Innovation & technical merit
   - Research methodology
   - Expected outcomes
   - Risk mitigation

3. **Budget** (detailed breakdown)
   - Personnel (45%)
   - Materials (25%)
   - Equipment (15%)
   - Travel (5%)
   - Other (5%)
   - Indirect costs (5%)

4. **Timeline** (20 months)
   - Phase 1: Planning (2 months)
   - Phase 2: Development (8 months)
   - Phase 3: Testing (4 months)
   - Phase 4: Commercialization (6 months)

5. **Team Qualifications**
   - Principal Investigator
   - Technical team
   - Advisory board
   - Key consultants

6. **Impact Analysis**
   - Technical impact
   - Economic impact
   - Social impact
   - Commercial impact

### Optimization Engine

**Analysis**:
- Compares against successful past applications
- Identifies common winning elements
- Detects weaknesses in proposal
- Suggests improvements

**Improvements**:
- Strengthened impact statements
- Enhanced competitive analysis
- Optimized budget allocation
- Improved team qualifications
- Added preliminary data

**Results**:
- Original score: 65/100
- Optimized score: 85/100
- Success probability increase: +20%
- Specific recommendations provided
