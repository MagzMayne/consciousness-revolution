## FAQ

### Q: Can I upgrade my tier later?
**A:** Yes! You can upgrade at any time by paying the difference. Contact BarbrickDesign@gmail.com for upgrade instructions.

### Q: How does account stacking work with revenue share?
**A:** Each account earns its own revenue share independently. If you have 10 Enterprise accounts, each can earn 30% from different projects.

### Q: Is the AI automation really hands-off?
**A:** For Ultimate tier, yes! The system can operate completely autonomously. For Diamond and Enterprise, some oversight is recommended.

### Q: What if I'm not satisfied?
**A:** Contact us within 30 days for a full refund if you haven't participated in any grant applications.

### Q: Can I share my account with a team?
**A:** Ultimate tier includes team collaboration features. For other tiers, each team member needs their own account.

### Q: How quickly can I start seeing revenue?
**A:** Grant funding timelines vary (typically 3-12 months), but active contributors typically see first earnings within 6 months.
