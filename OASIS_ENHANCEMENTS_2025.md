## 🚀 How to Use

### Starting the Game:
1. Open `oasis.html` in a modern browser
2. See the epic main menu with glowing title
3. Click "Enter OASIS" to start playing
4. Use WASD to move, mouse to look, click to attack

### Using the Marketplace:
1. Click "NFT Marketplace" from main menu or press M in-game
2. Browse available virtual assets
3. Click "Purchase" to buy (simulated transaction)
4. Items appear in your wallet panel

### Viewing the Map:
1. Click "World Map" from main menu or press Tab in-game
2. See your explored territory
3. Find quest objectives and points of interest
4. Plan your journey through the universe

### Connecting Wallet:
1. Click "Connect Wallet" in the wallet panel
2. If MetaMask is installed, it will connect
3. Otherwise, runs in demo mode
4. View your virtual assets in the panel

### Completing Quests:
1. Check the quest panel (top-right)
2. Complete objectives by playing
3. Get notified when quests complete
4. Earn XP and NFT rewards
