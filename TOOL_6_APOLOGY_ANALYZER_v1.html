Anatomy of a Real Apology

A genuine apology has 4 parts: (1) Acknowledgment of what was done - specifically, (2) Recognition of the impact - without minimizing, (3) Taking responsibility - without excuses, (4) Commitment to change - with concrete steps. Missing parts = incomplete apology.
